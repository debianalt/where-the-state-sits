**Figure 1.** Where the state is, and what its market looks like there.
Argentina's 24 subnational jurisdictions, 2019–2025. (a) The 137 seats of the
buying units, each sized by how many of the 456 located units sit there.
(b) The mean position of those units' suppliers on the axis of
access, from the personal pole in the north to the corporate one in the
federal capital; the capital, too small to see, is redrawn nine times larger
off the coast. *Source*: authors' elaboration on
COMPR.AR award records, the SIPRO supplier register and the unit gazetteer,
2019–2025.

**Figure 2.** The market in one plane. Correspondence analysis of the table
of ties between 456 buying units and 10,529 suppliers, 2019–2025; both axes
carry the same scale within a panel. (a) The 455 units with a located seat,
sized by their share of the awards, with the 24 jurisdictions of the seat at
the mean position of their units; eight units lie beyond the window drawn.
(b) The suppliers, drawn as the lattice of distinct positions sized by how
many suppliers share each and shaded from light to dark for natural persons,
other companies and public limited companies, with the 24 jurisdictions of
the fiscal domicile at the mean position of their suppliers; the window
holds 90 per cent of the suppliers and 960 lie beyond it. (c) The other
supplementary categories of both sides, each at the mean position of its
members: the units' anchoring and rank, and the suppliers' legal forms.
Distances are readable within each side and not between them. CABA is the
*Ciudad Autónoma de Buenos Aires*, the federal capital. *Source*: authors'
elaboration on COMPR.AR award records, the SIPRO supplier register and the
unit gazetteer, 2019–2025.

**Figure 3.** The homology at the level of the buying unit. (a) Each unit's
position on the axis built from the coordinates the supplier space never
sees (jurisdiction of the seat, distribution of the organ, seniority, rank,
budget and size) against the mean position of its suppliers on the axis of
access, with the 24 jurisdictions at the mean of their units, named in two
blocks according to the sign of that mean; the line is a
least-squares fit. (b) Within the seven organs of the within-organ test, the
difference in the mean access position of a unit's
suppliers between a seat in each province and a seat in the federal capital,
from a model with a fixed effect for each organ and the scale, procedure and
size of the unit held, with 95 per cent intervals. *Source*: authors'
elaboration on COMPR.AR award records, the SIPRO supplier register and the
unit gazetteer, 2019–2025.

**Figure 4.** Where the state names, in the plane of the ties. Share of
suppliers with at least one award granted on exclusivity, share with at least
one granted on speciality, and share exempted from competition on the amount
alone, by decile of (a) the first and (b) the second axis of the joint plane,
with 95 per cent Wilson intervals. *Source*: authors' elaboration on COMPR.AR
award records and the SIPRO supplier register, 2019–2025.
